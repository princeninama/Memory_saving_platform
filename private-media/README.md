# Private Media Directory

Files placed inside `private-media/` are **never** exposed publicly.
They are streamed exclusively through `/api/media/<relative-path>` after server-side passphrase authentication.

## Folder Structure

- `images/beach/` — Beach photos
- `images/college/` — College scrapbook photos & scratch-card images
- `images/delhi/` — Delhi street, cafe & evening photos
- `images/travel/` — Ghumi Ghumi trip photos
- `images/mountains/` — Hampta Pass & Tungnath sunrise photos
- `images/portraits/` — Portraits for "Through your eyes" / "Through my eyes"
- `images/album-art/` — Optional custom album cover images
- `videos/intro/` — Chapter 02 her smile video (e.g. `her-smile.mp4`)
- `videos/college/` — College clips
- `videos/travel/` — Travel clips
- `videos/delhi/` — Delhi daily clips
- `videos/mountains/` — Mountain clips
- `audio/her/` — Her voice recordings (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`)
- `audio/mine/` — Your voice recordings
- `audio/music/` — Optional personal ambient track (`.mp3`, `.wav`)
